CalculatorPro is a fast, free and completely offline scientific calculator.

Features:

* Basic and scientific modes
* sin, cos, tan, log, ln, square root, x squared, x to the y, pi, e, absolute value, factorial and percent
* Calculation history that is stored on your device and can be restored with a single tap
* Optional key press sounds and haptic feedback
* Long press AC clears the whole calculation at once
* A polished material design interface with a gradient theme
* Works completely offline - no ads, no tracking, no account required
* Keyboard support on tablets and Chromebooks

CalculatorPro is free and open source software, licensed under the MIT license.

The interface follows the Material Design 3 guidelines and adapts to the light and
dark theme of the device.
