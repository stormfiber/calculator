A four-mode (Basic, Standard, Professional, Scientific) Material 3 calculator.

Features:
* Paper-tape calculation history with search, date filters and undo
* Professional tools: TAX+/TAX-, markup, grand total, margin, discount and loan/EMI calculators
* Scientific functions: sin/cos/tan (+inverses), log/ln, factorial, PI/e, DEG/RAD
* Light / dark / OLED themes and 5 Material You palettes, all persisted
* No network access, no tracking, no ads
